How it works

We bring the device to you and help you every step of the way.

  1. 1

    Pick up to 3 devices

    Browse below, or call us and we will suggest some.

  2. 2

    We deliver and set up

    A volunteer brings them to your home and shows you how they work.

  3. 3

    Try for 4 weeks

    Use them in daily life. We check in after a few days.

  4. 4

    Keep, swap or return

    Your choice. Tell us what you thought to help other seniors.
